Predict from the title

  • What does “journey” suggest about this chapter?

Slide 5

Reading focus: setting

  1. Underline details that describe the land, plants and animals the family meets.
  2. Note one decision the family makes because of where they are.
  3. Mark any word you cannot define and use context clues: look at the words and sentences nearby.
  4. Record one quotation you could use as evidence later.
